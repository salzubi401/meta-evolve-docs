    Meta-evolution

    Status: Design—not shipped.

    This page records the target relationship between ordinary evaluated runs and future harness or policy artifacts. It defines no shipped classes.

    Inner and outer loops

    An outer candidate would describe a harness or policy. Its evaluator would run fresh, bounded inner meta.run() episodes against an experiment-controlled suite. Inner artifacts, trials, evaluations, failures, evidence, and usage would retain their own identities while linking to the outer evaluation.

    The candidate could change only a declared mutation surface. It could not change the suite, hidden splits, evaluator, enclosing budget, permissions, cache semantics, or promotion rule.

    Policy meta-evolution

    Two policy artifacts must be compared under compatible task distribution, model, evaluator, environment, seeds, and budgets. Aggregation must preserve uncertainty and typed failures rather than average missing measurements into low scores. Held-out results remain unavailable until the relevant evaluation permits them.

    Nested budgets

    Outer and inner evaluation, trial, token, and wall limits require explicit reservation and settlement. Inner exhaustion cannot consume undeclared outer authority. Cache reuse is valid only when every behavior-affecting declaration matches.

    M8 and M9 begin with decision-complete designs and reserved examples 20 and 21. See the M8 design and milestone roadmap.
